# US-4 — PvP shooting (local loop)

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| US-4.1 | LMB fires hitscan ray from camera center |
| US-4.2 | Fire rate ~2 shots/sec, range 100 m |
| US-4.3 | Hit resolves zone → applyDamage → health store |
| US-4.4 | Static dummy soldier target for local testing |
| US-4.5 | Eliminated player cannot move/shoot |
| US-4.6 | Respawn after 3 s at random spawn point |

## Acceptance

Player shoots dummy, zone damage applies, elimination + respawn works.
